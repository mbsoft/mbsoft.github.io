Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

ErgSoft is an open-source training app for Bluetooth rowing and cycling ergometers, published by Jim Welch (mbsoft), Dublin, Ohio, USA.

Summary. ErgSoft does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. It has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Data the app handles, and where it stays

  • Workout data (time, distance, pace, power, stroke rate, calories) received from your fitness machine over Bluetooth is stored only on your device.
  • Heart-rate data from your Apple Watch, a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor or Apple Health is shown during workouts and stored with the workout on your device.
  • If you grant permission, completed workouts are written to Apple Health. Apple Health data is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your Health settings.
  • Settings you enter (units, language, date of birth, weight, maximum heart rate, benchmarks) are stored on your device only.
  • The optional "send heart rate to console" feature broadcasts your current heart rate over Bluetooth to a fitness machine you choose to pair. It is off by default.

Data that leaves your device

None, unless you connect a third-party integration (such as Strava) in a future version. Each integration will ask for your consent and describe what it sends before it is enabled.

Permissions the app requests

  • Bluetooth — to connect to your fitness machine and heart-rate sensors.
  • Health — to read heart rate and to save workouts, if you choose.
  • Siri & Shortcuts — to start a session by voice or automation; nothing is sent to Apple beyond the standard Shortcuts processing.

Children

ErgSoft is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your choices

Delete any workout from the History screen. Deleting the app removes all its data from your device; Apple Health records remain in Health unless you delete them there.
