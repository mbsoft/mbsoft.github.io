Support

Questions, bugs and feature requests: open an issue on GitHub or email jwelch@mbsoft.com. Include the diagnostics text from Settings → About → Copy diagnostics if the problem is device-related.

Supported machines

Any rower or bike that advertises the Bluetooth Fitness Machine Service (FTMS), including the Rogue Echo Rower and Echo Bike. Bluetooth heart-rate straps and armbands using the standard Heart Rate Service are supported.

Common questions

The machine doesn't appear in Devices → Scan.
Wake the console so it advertises, make sure no other app (including the manufacturer's) is already connected to it, and tap Scan again. Only one app can be connected to the console at a time.
No heart rate from my Apple Watch.
Open ErgSoft on the watch and tap Start (or use the "Start erg session" Shortcut, which launches both). The watch must be unlocked and on your wrist. Any watch workout — including Apple's Workout app — also feeds heart rate to the phone through Apple Health with a few seconds of delay, if Health → Data Access → ErgSoft → Heart Rate is allowed.
Showing heart rate on the erg console.
Devices → "Send HR to erg console", then on the console choose Connect → Connect Heart Rate and pick ErgSoft HR. Keep ErgSoft on screen while pairing.
Workouts aren't in Apple Health.
Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → ErgSoft → allow Workouts and Heart Rate. Sessions finished before permission was granted can't be back-filled.
