ErgSoft

Open-source training app for Bluetooth rowers and bike ergs

OverviewSupportPrivacyGitHub

ErgSoft turns a Bluetooth rower or bike erg into a connected training station — no subscription, no account, no cloud. It pairs with the Rogue Echo Rower and Echo Bike and any FTMS machine, shows live pace, watts and stroke rate, runs interval workouts, streams heart rate from Apple Watch, and saves every session to Apple Health.

  • Live metrics straight from the console: time, meters, pace, watts, stroke rate, drag factor, calories, averages
  • Heart rate from Apple Watch or any Bluetooth strap — and sent back to the erg console so it shows there too
  • Just Row, distance and time pieces, 6×5 / 9×5 / 6×10 splits, 4×500m, Tabata
  • History with pinch-zoom charts; stats over 7/30/90 days with heart-rate zones; personal benchmarks
  • iPad first-person river view that scrolls at your real speed
  • Siri and Shortcuts, watch-face complication, metric/imperial, four languages, dark mode

Free and open source (GPL-3.0). App Store link coming after review.
